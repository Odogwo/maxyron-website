## 1. Decision and rationale

MAXYRON must present and operate as **an intelligent systems engineering, research and knowledge-systems organization**, **not an unrestricted multi-subject content website** and not an academy by default.

The scope of applicable knowledge can span almost every field of human learning and activity. That breadth is **input breadth**, not an obligation to publish articles or create applications for every subject. Subjects become relevant when they help MAXYRON solve problems, produce and deliver systems, enable evidence-grounded research, develop practical capabilities, or build verified learning/mastery experiences.

**North-star proposition:** MAXYRON is defined by what it enables people and organizations to accomplish through knowledge, intelligence, research, technology and systematic development—not by the number of subjects it covers.

The November 2 architecture's parent organizational positioning, existing four services, candidate product families and cautious hybrid integration model are retained. This note refines the relationships among **services, products, knowledge/research, and learning/mastery** and provides boundaries for new subject platforms. It does not imply legal corporate form, accreditation, production launches, or changes to live site navigation.

## 2. Four organizational functions and their boundaries

| Function | Question answered | Primary output | Ownership and accountability |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Services** | What work do we do for a client? | Scoped engagements, delivery, implementation, analysis, advisory | Client-specific commitments, evidence, handover |
| **Products** | What reusable system or solution do we build? | Diagnostics, tools, intelligent applications, domain platforms | Product-owned repository, releases, tests, support |
| **Learning & Mastery** | What capability can a person demonstrate? | Guided instruction, practice, assessment, retrieval, transfer, evidence of unaided performance | Domain curriculum, competence claims and evaluation |
| **Knowledge & Research** | What reliable intellectual foundation do the others use? | Canonical concepts, scholarly evidence, interpretations, analyses, articles and knowledge models | Provenance, domain authority, evidence labels, review and versioning |

These are **functions**, not four equal legal companies or necessarily four top-level website navigation tabs. They can share infrastructure and operate across domains while retaining different responsibilities. A research article is not automatically a product; a training note is not proof of mastery; a prototype is not a market-ready offering; contracted client work is not automatically a reusable product.

**Operational flow:** Knowledge & Research can inform learning experiences, reusable products and services. Learning can build user capability; products can embody capability; services can apply capability. Feedback from every function may refine the knowledge base.

## 4. Subject breadth without subject sprawl

Potential domains: AI, Bible, spiritual consciousness, religion, philosophy, psychology, language, mathematics, science, learning science, personal development and wider human/work activity.

A subject is **not** automatically a new business division, product, repository, or website. Decide using:
- Clear audience, use case, and governing question.
- Relationship to at least one organizational function above.
- Existing canonical owner/source and overlap audit.
- Whether a bounded knowledge object, module, product, or standalone platform is justified.
- Verified demand/capability and resource/maintenance implications.
- Ethical and evidentiary boundaries (religious interpretation vs psychological research vs personal reflection).

Default: **connect and reuse before creating**; share reusable platform capabilities only after validated reuse needs. Maintain authoritative domain ownership rather than replicating data across repositories.

### Article evolution methodology

One durable governing question → one canonical article → controlled revisions when new insights, mechanisms, examples, counterarguments or evidence improve it. Preserve identity, human-readable voice, useful distinctions and near-stable length by **replacement/rebalancing**, not endless additions. Record version history, evidence status and why each revision changed. Create a separate article only for a sufficiently distinct governing question.

Initial case: **Prayer and the Practice of Spiritual Living**, combining the function of prayer, intrusive thoughts/rumination, the exercise-discomfort analogy, spiritual orientation, meaningful engagement, and living well amid unresolved problems.

## 6. Proposed public information architecture (not yet implemented)

A corporate-first presentation:
- **Company** — identity, mission, team, trust and governance
- **Expertise / Services** — client delivery capabilities and engagement pathways
- **Solutions / Products** — only suitably verified applications, categorized by purpose and readiness
- **Research & Knowledge** — selected evidence-grounded insights and methodological work supporting the organization
- **Learning & Mastery** — approved programmes and tools with clear audience and mastery evidence
- **Contact** — enquiries, engagements and collaboration

This is an information architecture proposal, not authorization to alter current live navigation. Keep public-facing commercial clarity: a visitor should quickly understand what MAXYRON does, for whom, what can be bought or engaged, and what is experimental or educational.

## 7. Future development pattern

1. **Verify present state**: inspect current corporate site, integration registry and canonical domain repositories.
2. **Classify** all proposed offerings by function (service/product/knowledge/learning), audience, owner, proof and maturity.
3. **Select a narrow pilot**: spiritual article evolution or an already working mastery product; do not rebuild entire ecosystem at once.
4. **Develop** standalone domain-specific components with authority boundaries and versioned evidence.
5. **Reuse** proven shared identity, retrieval, editorial, practice, assessment and analytics components only when warranted.
6. **Publish** only verified capabilities on corporate site with accurate labels and trustworthy links.
7. **Review the strategy** when organizational scope, revenue model, audience or evidence changes.

Questions intentionally open: final external naming of the spiritual platform; whether Learning & Mastery deserves a named sub-brand; whether the corporate navigation needs a Products page; which applications qualify for public listing; deployment/subdomain architecture; commercial model; distinct domain data authorities.
