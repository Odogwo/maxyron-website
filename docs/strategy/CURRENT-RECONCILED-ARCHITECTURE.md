## 1. One current authority; earlier versions preserved

**MAXYRON is an intelligent systems engineering and research organization.** The retained tagline is **Engineering Intelligent Systems.** It designs, researches, develops, and delivers intelligent applications, automation, knowledge architectures, evidence-based advisory and learning technologies. It is **not** a general content directory, nor does its learning portfolio make it an accredited academy.

**Reconciled distinction:** broad subject coverage is the source material and capability territory, not the organization type. An item becomes a public MAXYRON offering only when it has a defined audience, value, owner, verified maturity and appropriate evidence.

There are four organizational **functions**—not necessarily separate subsidiaries or equal nav items:

| Function | Meaning | Typical outputs | Public label proposed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Services | Bespoke work for a specific client | Scoping, consulting, development, research, delivery | Services |
| Products | Reusable systems that MAXYRON creates | AI diagnostics, research tools, knowledge applications | Products |
| Learning & Mastery | Acquiring and demonstrating capabilities | Courses, practice, retrieval, tests, transfer and competency evidence | Learning |
| Knowledge & Research | Intellectual/evidentiary foundation | Articles, research, principles, verified sources, models | Research or Insights |

**Relationship:** Research/knowledge informs all; services solve client-specific problems; products package reusable solutions; learning/mastery develops human capabilities. One domain may support several functions. A subject is never automatically a standalone service/product/site.

## 2. Current public services — retain exact labels and order

These are **already represented in current website source**, not a future taxonomy:

1. **AI Engineering** — custom AI applications, RAG, agents, evaluations, deployment.
2. **Intelligent Automation** — workflows, integration, approvals, exception handling.
3. **Knowledge Architecture** — repositories, taxonomies, ontologies, retrieval, governance.
4. **Research & Advisory** — academic and applied research, evidence review, data/methodology analysis, diagnosis, feasibility and decision support.

**Why Research & Advisory:** research creates a defensible understanding; advisory helps determine what action makes sense. This is client-facing and *not identical* to the internal/public Knowledge & Research function. Engineering may implement solutions informed by research. The exact rationale and boundaries are in `../SERVICE-OFFERS.md` and `../BRAND-DECISIONS.md`.

Website/software design may be offered as an **implementation capability within AI Engineering/Intelligent Automation or as a scoped software-delivery service**, but do not silently add a fifth headline service without evaluating audience, evidence, pricing and scope. Cybersecurity remains a **candidate capability/learning domain**, **not an advertised specialist security service** without qualifications, secure delivery capacity and evidence.

- Preserve homepage's four service labels and their order.

### NEXT — recommended navigation design, **not yet implemented**

- **Home:** what the organization does, for whom, method, credible proof and next action.
- **Services:** exactly four existing service groups, with clear client problems/deliverables and contact path.
- **Products:** separate verified apps/demonstrations from prototypes and candidate concepts; product detail pages may be phased in.
- **Learning:** approved learning/mastery systems and programmes, initially links/catalogue rather than a new school/SSO.

If maintaining a lean launch, Products/Learning/Research can initially be sections or a combined landing page rather than immediate full routes. No navigation change is authorized solely by this document.

### LATER — conditional
Shared mastery infrastructure, identity/progress, multi-domain dashboards, independent subdomains, CMS, marketplace/monetization, unified AI agent, richer research library. Implement only after validating reuse, safety, costs, maintenance and demand.

## 5. Placement rules for a new idea

1. Is it a **client-specific engagement**? → Services; route to one of the four service groups.
2. Is it a **reusable application/tool/system**? → Products, classed by its primary user problem and readiness.
3. Is it a **programme for demonstrated capability**? → Learning & Mastery.
4. Is it **evidence, an article, a reflection, a concept, or research**? → Knowledge & Research, with domain source provenance.
5. Is it a **cross-functional technique** (AI, evaluation, memory, software, security)? → shared capability or linked domain knowledge, **not automatically a new division**.
6. Audit existing canonical sources and repositories before creating a new article, platform or repo. Cross-tag rather than duplicate.
7. Record owner, stage, intended audience, public status and next verification step.

**Domain-specific boundaries:** `maxyron-website` owns parent public navigation and integration. `Elite_KOS` owns applicable governance/origination. `Bible-OS` owns Bible-specific content subject to migration. `maxyron-ai` is the recent canonical AI project; older `maxyron-ai-hub` needs authority reconciliation. Proposed spiritual repo owns interdisciplinary essays and their governed evolution, not a duplicate Bible-OS.
