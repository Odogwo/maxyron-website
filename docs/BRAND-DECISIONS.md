# Brand, Positioning and Decisions
Snapshot: 2026-10-31.

## Naming conventions
- **MAXYRON:** logo, wordmark, major graphic branding.
- **Maxyron:** professional correspondence, email display name, ordinary prose.
- **maxyron:** URL, DNS and technical identifiers.
- Tagline: **Engineering Intelligent Systems.**
- Domain: `maxyron.com`.
- General contact: `hello@maxyron.com`.
- Personal professional contact: `martins@maxyron.com`.

## Positioning
A systems-oriented business combining AI engineering, automation, knowledge architecture, research and consulting, and eventually digital products. The website currently calls MAXYRON an 'emerging intelligent systems engineering brand'; do not claim unverified clients or results.

## Legal/business relationship

## Decisions and rationale
- Domain-based email instead of personal Gmail for public contact: professional consistency.
- Zoho Mail Forever Free selected to minimize recurring cost at launch.
- Alias `hello@` instead of a separate user: one inbox, no additional license consumption.
- Vercel + GitHub for straightforward Next.js deployment.
- Keep implementation and project documentation together in GitHub, but keep internal documentation out of public website routes.
